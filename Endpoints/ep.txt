Fetch Orders:
http://localhost:8080/api/orders/user/${userId} (GET)


Fetch Cart:
http://localhost:8080/api/carts/user/${userId} (GET)


Fetch Inventory:
http://localhost:8080/api/inventories/users/${userId} (GET)


Add Product to Cart:
http://localhost:8080/api/carts/${user.cart.id}/items/add/${groceryId} (POST)


Delete Product from Cart:
http://localhost:8080/api/carts/${user.cart.id}/items/remove/${groceryId} (DELETE)


Update Product Quantity in Cart:
http://localhost:8080/api/carts/${user.cart.id}/items/modify/${groceryId}?quantity=${quantity} (PUT)


Toggle Product Favourite in Cart:
http://localhost:8080/api/carts/${user.cart.id}/items/${groceryId}/favorite (PUT)


Place Order from Cart:
http://localhost:8080/api/carts/order/${user.cart.id} (POST)


Update Inventory Quantity:
http://localhost:8080/api/inventories/${user.inventory.id}/items/${groceryId}?quantity=${quantity} (PUT)


Remove Item from Inventory:
http://localhost:8080/api/inventories/${user.inventory.id}/items/${groceryId} (DELETE)


http://localhost:8080/api/recipes (GET): Fetches all recipes.


http://localhost:8080/api/mealplans/range (GET): Fetches meal plans for a date range.


http://localhost:8080/api/mealplans (POST): Creates or updates a meal plan.


http://localhost:8080/api/orders/add/${userId} (POST): Creates a new order for the user id
